# Research Notes

## Objective

Prepare a binary classification dataset suitable for training a
4-qubit Variational Quantum Classifier.

## Dataset

MNIST

Selected classes:
- 0
- 1

## Preprocessing

- Min-Max normalization
- Stratified train-test split
- PCA (4 principal components)

## Reason for PCA

Each principal component is mapped to one qubit, allowing efficient
quantum feature encoding while reducing computational complexity.

## Output

Saved processed dataset for all subsequent experiments.
