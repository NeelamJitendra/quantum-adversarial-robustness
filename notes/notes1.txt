This is very important.

Quantum computers cannot efficiently process 784 features with a small number of qubits.

For our initial VQC, we'll reduce the dimensionality to 4 principal components, allowing us to encode the data into a 4-qubit circuit.'

Why PCA?

A common question in your thesis defense will be:

"Why did you reduce MNIST to only four features?"

Your answer should be:

A VQC with one feature per qubit requires one qubit for each encoded feature.
Current NISQ devices have limited reliable qubit counts and circuit depth.
Reducing the data to four principal components enables efficient simulation while preserving the most significant variance in the dataset.
This approach is consistent with many published QML studies that use dimensionality reduction before quantum feature encoding.
